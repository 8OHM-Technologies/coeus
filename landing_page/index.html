Infinity Ohm FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How do the Consumer and B2B divisions relate?

Both divisions are built on the same core philosophy: decoupling digital obsolescence from physical hardware, maximizing offline privacy, and building local intelligence bridges. The Consumer division empowers individuals with blueprints and retail hardware, while the B2B division delivers enterprise-grade IT/AI consultancy and custom data engineering.

What is your long-term hardware plan?

While we currently supply curated edge servers, edge nodes, and IoT components alongside our self-hosting guides, our long-term roadmap includes engineering our own proprietary, modular, open-source hardware retrofitting devices.
