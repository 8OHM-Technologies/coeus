Technical Questions & Answers

Clear, engineering-focused answers to common architectural and operational queries.

Why build custom pipelines instead of subscribing to a data feed?

Project COEUS gives you absolute ownership over your extraction parameters. It enables immediate adaptation to target schema changes without relying on third-party schedules or exposing search criteria.

Is offline-only hardware complex to maintain without cloud updates?

No. Our modules utilize local OTA (Over-the-Air) network updates with fail-safe recovery modes to ensure robust, secure updates over your LAN, keeping your smart building telemetry completely isolated from the internet.

Does the CAPTCHA solver require continuous manual training?

No. `misstcha` utilizes zero-shot vision-language models (Grounding DINO + Qwen) to interpret prompts and detect objects dynamically, avoiding the need for constant re-training when prompt styles change.

How is data sovereignty guaranteed on enterprise perimeters?

All scraped outputs, orchestration databases, model weights, and telemetry logs remain within customer-controlled infrastructure. The system executes zero external phone-home requests.
